NOTED Point: 
int ----(convert)-------->float, complex, string, Boolean.
Float---(convert)-------->int, complex, string, Boolean.
Complex-(convert)-------->Not Convert any Type.
string--(convert)-------->wherever defined numeric in the string that will be convert into (int, Float, complex) otherwise not convert & list, set, Tuple, Boolean.
list----(convert)-------->string, tuple, set, Boolean.
Tuple---(convert)-------->string, Boolean.
Set-----(convert)-------->String, Tuple, Boolean.
Dictionary-(convert)-------->Not Convert any type expect tuple, boolean.
Boolean-(convert)-------->int, Float, Complex.
